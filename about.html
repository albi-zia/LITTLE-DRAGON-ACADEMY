The Academy

Two Disciplines,
One Practice

Little Dragon Martial Arts Academy trains hard-style Chinese Kung-Fu and Wushu alongside Yoga — examined, graded, and taught under one roof in Ayarkunnam, Kottayam.

Our Approach

Hard Style, Soft Practice

Kung-Fu and Wushu build the strike — stances, forms, and self-defense, drilled toward examined black-belt duan grading. Yoga builds the stillness underneath it — breath, flexibility, and calm. We teach both to the same students, on the same floor, examined by the same certifying instructors who lead the academy.

Every rank at Little Dragon is tested, not assumed. Our President serves as Chief Examiner, holding a Kung-Fu IV Duan black belt and Wushu instructor credentials — grading here follows a real, examined standard.
