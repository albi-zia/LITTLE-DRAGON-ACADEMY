The Academy

Two Disciplines, One Practice

Kung-Fu and Wushu build the strike — stances, forms, and self-defense, drilled toward examined black-belt duan grading. Yoga builds the stillness underneath it — breath, flexibility, and calm. Little Dragon teaches both under one roof, to students of every age, examined by the same certifying instructors who lead the academy.

Examined Grading Rank is tested and certified by our own Chief Examiner, not assumed.
Hard + Soft Kung-Fu and Wushu strike. Yoga steadies. Both are part of the same curriculum.
All Ages Kids, teens, and adults train side by side on the same floor.
